Controls

Chapter 2 - Math
Mouse: Press and hold Left mouse button.

Chapter 3 - Steering Behaviours
Keyboard: Arrow keys to move around.
		  Space Bar to fire (Hold for continuous firing)

Chapter 4 - Terrain Analysis
On Screen Buttons
Spawn - Spawns a new alien at home.
Regenerate Grid - Regereates a new environment.

Chapter 5 - Pathfinding
- Use on Screen buttons to set up an environment. It is possible to place the start, end and obstales yourself, or to randomly generate one.
- Use on screen buttons to choose an algorithm, then select Stepped Search or Full Search to see the algorithm solve the problem.

Chapter 6 - Decision Making
Keyboard: Arrow keys to move around.
	  Press 1 - Finite State Machine approach
	  Press 2 - Behaviour Tree approach
	  Press 3 - Stack-based approach.

Chapter 7 - Fuzzy Logic
Mouse: Hold Left button down to aim.
	   Release Left button to enter Power stage.
	   Hold Right button until desired power level reached.

Chapter 8 - Chess Ai
Mouse: 	Select a piece with left button.
	Select a position to move to with Left button.

Chapter 9 - Genetic Algorithms
Keyboard: Left & Right arrow keys to rotate.
	  Space Bar to thrust.

Chapter 10 - Neural Networks
Keyboard: Arrow keys to move around.
  	  Space Bar to pause game.
